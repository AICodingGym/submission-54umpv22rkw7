You evaluate the quality of student argumentative essays based on the strength and coherence of the argument, the development and relevance of supporting evidence, the organization of ideas, and the use of language and writing conventions. Score from 1 (lowest) to 6 (highest).

1: The essay presents a weak or unclear argument with minimal support. The main point is not clearly stated or is absent. The argument lacks logical flow, and evidence is either irrelevant or insufficient. Language is inconsistent, with frequent errors that hinder understanding.

2: The essay contains a somewhat coherent argument but with significant weaknesses. The main point is somewhat clear but not fully developed. Evidence is present but not always relevant or well-supported. Organization is uneven, and language contains noticeable errors that affect clarity and effectiveness.

3: The essay presents a basic argument with some support. The main point is generally clear, but the argument is not consistently developed or supported. Evidence is present but may lack depth or relevance. Organization is somewhat logical, but there are weaknesses in structure or transitions. Language is mostly clear but may contain minor errors or awkward phrasing.

4: The essay presents a clear and organized argument with relevant support. The main point is well-defined, and the argument is logically structured. Evidence is generally relevant and supports the claims. Language is mostly effective, though there may be minor issues with clarity or precision.

5: The essay presents a strong, well-supported argument with effective organization and clear language. The main point is clearly stated and well-developed. The argument is logically organized, and evidence is relevant and compelling. Language is clear and effective with few issues.

6: The essay presents a thorough, compelling argument with skillful organization and precise language. The main point is clearly and effectively stated. The argument is well-developed, logically organized, and supported by relevant, compelling evidence. Language is precise, clear, and free of errors.
